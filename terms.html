05 Ordering & Pre-Dispatch Settlement Terms

5.1. Proforma Invoice Confirmation: Purchase orders are confirmed upon the issuance of an official Proforma Invoice (PI) by Mass Distributors detailing SKU carton counts, applicable tier margins, GST, and net payable value.

5.2. Advance Settlement Against Proforma: To maintain strict supply chain efficiency and secure optimal wholesale pricing, Mass Distributors operates on a streamlined Pre-Dispatch Settlement (Advance Against Proforma) model. Full payment of the Proforma Invoice value must be remitted in INR via RTGS, NEFT, or IMPS to the designated bank account of M/s. Mass Distributors within two (2) working days of Proforma Invoice generation. Orders are scheduled for priority dispatch immediately upon realization of cleared funds.

06 Dispatch, Logistics, Delivery, and Risk Transfer

6.1. Priority Dispatch SLA: Following payment realization against the Proforma Invoice, orders are scheduled for dispatch within 24 to 48 working hours from our Chithode, Erode central warehouse hub.

6.2. Transfer of Risk & Delivery Terms: Unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing on the Proforma Invoice (such as Ex-Godown delivery), delivery shall be executed via authorized Goods Transport Agencies (GTA) or logistics partners. Risk of loss, damage, or pilferage of goods passes entirely to the Partner upon dispatch and handover of the consignment to the carrier at the Erode warehouse hub.

6.3. Inspection & Shortage Claims Protocol:

  • The Partner or their designated receiving agent must inspect outer master cartons and verify carton counts against the Lorry Receipt (LR) and Tax Invoice immediately upon unloading.
  • Any apparent transit damage, carton crushing, or quantity shortage must be explicitly endorsed in writing on the carrier's Delivery Challan / LR and reported to Mass Distributors within twenty-four (24) hours of delivery, accompanied by unboxing photographs and video evidence. Failure to report within 24 hours shall constitute a waiver of transit claims.
  • No Return of Salable Stock: Consignments delivered in sound condition in accordance with confirmed orders are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable.

07 Warehousing, FSSAI Compliance, and Stock Hygiene

7.1. Storage Conditions: Partners must store all food and spice SKUs in a cool, dry, hygienic, pest-free, and well-ventilated godown away from moisture and direct sunlight, in strict compliance with FSSAI regulations.

7.2. FEFO / FIFO Stock Rotation: Partners must enforce First-Expiry, First-Out (FEFO) stock movement. Mass Distributors bears no liability for stock expiry caused by poor inventory rotation at the Partner's facility.

7.3. Prohibition on Tampering: All retail packs (25g–300g) and institutional HORECA packs (up to 5kg) must be sold in their original sealed packaging. Tampering with batch codes, expiry dates, MRP, or FSSAI labels, or repackaging bulk packs into smaller loose units, is strictly prohibited and subject to legal prosecution.

7.4. Warehouse Audits: Mass Distributors and representatives of our Master Importer reserve the right to inspect Partner warehouses and audit stock/sales records during business hours.
