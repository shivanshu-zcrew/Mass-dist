How this estimate is calculated

  • Cartons, price, margin, and stock-hold days are the numbers you provide above.
  • Turnover, profit, working capital, ROI, and payback are calculated directly from those four — nothing else feeds the result.
  • Working capital = cost of goods sold × (stock-hold days ÷ 30) — you're assumed to carry capital only for as long as stock sits before resale.
  • ROI is annualized gross profit ÷ working capital — a return-on-capital measure, not a bank-guaranteed yield.
  • Excludes rent, staff, transport, and other operating costs. Assumes resale at your input price. No credit or bad-debt risk is modelled, since none exists under a 100% cash & carry model.

Figures are estimates based on the inputs and assumptions above — not a guarantee of income, profit, or return.
